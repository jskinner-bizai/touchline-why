# Application rights and license status

Confirmation of the application rights remains pending. This staged build does not invent an application copyright ownership assertion or redistribution grant. The owner may choose an MIT license, but the competition rules do not prescribe MIT. A public repository alone does not grant an open-source license.

Dependencies retain their original terms. Their notices are preserved under `third_party/`; `DEPENDENCY_MANIFEST.json` records the declared licenses and hashes of the wheels and notices. The framework's MIT license does not license the original application. Dependency terms include MIT, BSD, Apache, MPL and historical Python license terms.

Model terms are separate. The included provenance and license files document the historical local model experiment. No model weights are included. Historical receipts describe test activity, without establishing contest eligibility, a submission or a result.

The existing local FFmpeg recording tool and its encoder binaries are excluded. The remaining application rights confirmation concerns ownership or permission for the submitted code, synthetic assets and recorded outputs, and permission to make the competition materials public. This status note does not resolve that confirmation.
